Splash Bros — internal field-service platform

Sole engineer for my brother's pool service company in Las Vegas. Built the customer site and the internal platform the business runs on: 50–60 recurring accounts serviced weekly, in production since 2024.

Problem
Every service visit generated paperwork: chemical logs, photos, billing, customer notifications. It ate roughly 40 administrative hours a month.
Built
A mobile workflow technicians complete on-site — chemical tracking, service documentation with photos, billing, and customer notifications fire automatically. Stripe billing handles ~$7K/month in payments.
Results
